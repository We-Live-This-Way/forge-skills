# Find local business leads on Facebook

Goal: a short, verified list of local businesses that likely need the service being sold, each with a public source link and a reason. Quality over volume.

## Ground rules
- Use a real, signed-in Facebook account in a normal browser, at human pace (a few pages a minute, a few dozen businesses per session). No scrapers, bots that bulk-collect, or unofficial APIs; Facebook's terms forbid automated collection and bans the account.
- Read only public info: business Pages, public posts, public group posts, Marketplace and Services listings.
- Never message, friend, comment, join-and-spam, or post without the owner's explicit OK for that exact message. Outreach happens later, by a person or through a channel with proper consent.
- Collect business contact info only (business name, page URL, business phone/email/website as published). No private personal profiles, no personal phone numbers from friends lists.
- For any texting campaign afterwards: TCPA and A2P 10DLC rules apply. A number found on Facebook is not consent to text; cold outreach should be a call, email, or DM the owner approves.

## 1. Define the target first
Write down: the service being sold, the business types it fits (e.g. groomers, salons, barbers, auto detailers, cleaners, contractors, clinics), the area (cities/ZIP list), and the buying signals to look for.

Good buying signals for appointment-reminder / AI front desk / missed-call offers:
- Posts like "call or text to book", "DM to book", "sorry we missed your call", "booked up, message us".
- Reviews or comments complaining about no-shows, slow replies, unanswered calls.
- Hiring posts for a receptionist / front desk.
- No online booking link on the Page, or booking only by phone.
- Active page (posted in the last 30 days) with real customers commenting.

## 2. Where to look
1. Facebook search: "<business type> <city>" then the Pages filter; sort through results, open each Page.
2. Local groups: "<city> small business", "<city> moms", "<county> recommendations". Search inside the group for the business type; businesses recommended there are active and trusted.
3. Marketplace / Services: search the service category within the area.
4. Cross-check each candidate on Google Maps (hours, rating, review count, website) to confirm it is real and local.

## 3. Qualify each business (keep only fits)
For every candidate record:
- Business name, type, city
- Facebook Page URL (and website / Google Maps link if found)
- Published business phone/email (as shown publicly)
- Last post date
- Signal(s) seen, with a link to the post or review
- Fit score 1-3 (3 = clear signal + active + no booking system)
Drop chains/franchises with corporate systems, closed or inactive pages, and anything outside the area.

## 4. Save and hand off
- Save as a CSV (one row per business, columns as above) or import into the CRM as new leads with source "Facebook" and the signal in notes. Dedupe against existing leads by phone and Page URL first.
- Report: count found, count qualified, top 10 by fit score with links, and any patterns (e.g. "groomers in Hialeah mostly book by DM").
- Do not start outreach. Offer drafted first-touch messages for the owner to review and send.

## 5. Repeat safely
Run one business type + one area per session. Note which searches and groups produced good leads so the next session starts there. If Facebook shows a checkpoint, captcha you can't pass, or a warning about activity, stop and tell the owner.
